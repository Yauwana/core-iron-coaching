Questions

I live outside Australia. Can you still coach me?

Yes. Online coaching runs over message and video, so time zone and country make no difference. Clients anywhere are welcome.

I've never trained before. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most people start there. We build technique and confidence first, then add load.

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

No. Nutrition guidance is built around what you already eat, so it is something you can stick to.

What happens on the free consult?

We talk through your goals, training history, schedule and any injuries, and I tell you honestly which format suits you and what it costs.
